Install on iPhone

Only Safari can install apps to your Home Screen on iPhone — this is an Apple restriction that applies to every website, not something any page can bypass. If you opened this link inside another app (Messages, Instagram, Gmail, etc.), tap its "•••" or share menu first and choose "Open in Safari" before following the steps below.

1
Open this page in Safari (the blue compass icon).
2
Tap the Share icon — a square with an arrow pointing up — in the toolbar (bottom on iPhone, top on iPad).
3
Scroll down the list of actions until you see "Add to Home Screen." Tap it.
4
Tap "Add" in the top right. The app icon now appears on your Home Screen.
Tap the browser's share icon and choose "Add to Home Screen" to open this like a regular app. Everything you enter is saved automatically on this device. Prices you add to an activity are automatically logged as expenses for that day.
